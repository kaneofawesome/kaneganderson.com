This is, to me, what Tyler Cowen calls a 'quake book'. It shakes your ideas of the world and opens your eyes to a new way of thinking. In the opening chapter the author offers that the Carnegie secret to wealth can be uncovered by those ready to receive it. The secret is not easily described, and which the author named the 'master key'. I will not do you the injustice of paraphrasing my own short description. You will be better served to read it yourself, and God willing you receive the alluded understanding. You will have an awakening of desire and adopt (or further develop) a definite purpose.

Everything in Think and Grow Rich is worth repeating, alas this post will not suffice. ## 8. Decision

Success requires no explanation. Failure permits no alibis

Keep your own counsel. Reach decisions and follow them. Don't allow others to make your decisions for you.

## 12. The Subconscious Mind

You may voluntarily plant in your subconscious mind any plan, thought, or purpose which you desire to translate into its physical or monetary equivalent.
