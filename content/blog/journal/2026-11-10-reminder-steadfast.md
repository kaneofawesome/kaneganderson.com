# Be Steadfast

In all circumstances it is within your power to choose wisdom, integrity, benevolent, loving, and disciplined action. Does your soul command you? It speaks with your own voice through your conscience. That is your divine spark and your connection to God. Your animal nature will find self-absorbed reasons to shirk responsibility out of laziness or cowardice. To "man up" is to forge ahead and obey your conscience, and through right action you obey God. To remain in this state of integrity true to your personal values, and morality by which you have reasoned and judged to be good, can be summarized in one word - steadfast.
