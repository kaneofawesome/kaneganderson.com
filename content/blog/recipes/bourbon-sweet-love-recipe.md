Here is the recipe for one of my signature cocktails. Invented in Florida May '24. It's called the Bourbon sweet love.

- 3 pcs cantelope muddled

- 1.5 oz. Bourbon

- 1 oz. Simple syrup

- 0.5 oz. Cinnamon whiskey like Tennessee fire or fireball

Shake with ice

- 1 pc cantelope garnish

Serve with ice
