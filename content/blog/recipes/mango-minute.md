Here is the recipe for another cocktail I invented. It's nice.

- 3 chunks of mango, muddled

- 2 oz. brandy

- 1 oz. triple sec

- 1 oz. simple syrup

- 1 dash of citrus bitters

Shake all ingredients with ice, serve with ice. Use a chunk of mango for garnish.
