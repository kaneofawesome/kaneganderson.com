Here is the recipe for a twist on another cocktail, like the mango minute but more passionate

- 1/2 oz of passionfruit juice

- 2 oz. brandy

- 1 oz. Cointreau

- 1 oz. simple syrup

- 1 dash of angostura bitters

- 1 dash of lime juice

Shake all ingredients with ice, serve with ice. Use a chunk of passionfruit for garnish.
