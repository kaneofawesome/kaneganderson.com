Here is the recipe for a twist on another cocktail, like the mango minute but more passionate

- 3 chunks of passionfruit , muddled

- 2 oz. brandy

- 1 oz. Cointreau

- 1 oz. simple syrup

- 1 dash of citrus bitters

Shake all ingredients with ice, serve with ice. Use a chunk of mango for garnish.
